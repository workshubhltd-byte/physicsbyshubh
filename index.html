      About PhysicsByShubh

      Physics ko ratna nahi — dekhna aur samajhna.

      PhysicsByShubh is a small, independent project by Shubh Bhagat. Everything here is built around one idea: a student should be able to see the physics before memorising the formula.

      Every model on this site is interactive. You change the mass, the distance, the frequency or the angle, and the result updates immediately — with the formula and the numbers shown next to it. Where an animation is slowed down for clarity, the page says so.

      What is covered

      • Class 9 · Gravitation — Newton's law, free fall, mass vs weight, weight on other planets
      • Class 9 · Sound — production, medium, longitudinal waves, speed in media, echo, pitch and loudness
      • Physics Lab — projectile, pendulum, inclined plane, solar system, wave motion, "build your own planet"
      • Practice — solved numericals with step-by-step 3D explanations and read-aloud support

      How it is made

      Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Three.js — no trackers beyond basic analytics, no autoplay video, and it works on a low-end phone. The site loads in a couple of seconds on 4G because there is no heavy framework.

      Contact

      For doubts, corrections or ideas: contact page. If you spot a wrong number or a badly worded question, tell me — I fix those first.

      Privacy Policy

      Last updated: 2026

      What we collect

      • Nothing, by default. You can use the 3D models, the lab and the free questions without an account.
      • If you sign in: your name and email address, stored in Supabase (our authentication provider).
      • If you submit a doubt or request notes: the name, class, chapter, question and email you type into the form. Forms are processed by Netlify.
      • On your device: your progress, streak and XP are saved in your browser's localStorage. Clearing browser data removes them.

      Third parties

      • Netlify — hosting and form submissions
      • Supabase — sign-in (email one-time code)
      • Google Fonts — fonts; cdnjs / jsDelivr — the Three.js library
      • Analytics (optional) — if enabled, it may collect anonymous page counts; Google may use cookies to personalise them. You can opt out at adssettings.google.com

      Children

      The site is meant for school students. We do not ask for a phone number, address or date of birth, and we do not run profiles of individual students.
